How this is scored

Every player gets one score built from three things: recent form, how easy their next fixture is, and a small bump for playing at home. No black box — the number next to each name on the right is that total, high to low.

Players who are injured, suspended, or barely playing are left off the list entirely.

Data from the official Fantasy Premier League API. Refreshed every 10 minutes.
